Common Questions

Before you enroll.

How is this different from other personal-growth programs?

Most programs work on results — habits, productivity, mindset tweaks. This one works at the root: the inherited belief that holds the whole chain in place. Change that, and thoughts, emotions, actions, and results reorganize on their own. It draws on four pillars — the psychology of change, plant-based medicine, Jewish philosophy, and Kabbalah & energy.

How long does it take?

It's self-paced. Most people move through the eight modules over six to ten weeks, but the material is yours for life — you can go faster, slower, or revisit any module whenever you need it.

What's the difference between Core and Plus?

Core is the complete self-paced program. Plus adds a live monthly group Q&A with Jacob and a private community, so you're moving through the work alongside others and can get your specific questions answered.

Is this therapy?

No. Therapy works with what's wounded; this works with what's next. If you're in active crisis, start with a therapist. If you're stable but stuck against a ceiling you can't name, this is the right tool.

What if I want to work with Jacob one-on-one?

That's the 1:1 Coaching path. It begins with a free discovery call and is offered by application to a small number of clients.
