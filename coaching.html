The Mechanism

We work in the gap.

There's a gap between the story you've been living and the one that's trying to emerge. Most people stand at the edge of it for years. Coaching is the bridge — structured, honest, and built for your specific crossing.

A program teaches you the map. Coaching walks the territory with you — naming the belief, holding the discomfort, and keeping you moving when the old pattern pulls back.

How We Work

Three phases. No shortcuts.

An engagement unfolds over a multi-month arc, at the pace your life can metabolize.

01

Excavation

We map the layers — the beliefs, roles, and narratives that aren't actually yours. We find where the ceiling formed and what it has cost you.

02

Reorientation

We work in the gap between the old story and the new one. This is where the shift happens — not a rebrand, a return to the self that was always there.

03

Integration

The new orientation flows into your seven life domains — work, body, relationships, time, spirit, money, contribution — as an expression of who you actually are.
