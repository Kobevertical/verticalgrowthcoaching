About

Jacob V

I help people in the middle of their lives move through the ceiling they can't see — and build the chapter that's actually theirs.

"The gap between your old story and your new one. That's exactly where the work happens."

I've spent years studying what happens to people in the middle of their lives — when the external scaffolding that held a certain version of them up starts to move. Careers shift. Kids leave. Parents age. A milestone passes and something quietly cracks. The life looks right from the outside, and still there's a ceiling overhead that no amount of climbing seems to clear.

What I found, again and again, is that the ceiling isn't made of circumstance. It's made of inherited belief — and belief runs a chain that produces the same results on repeat. Work on the results and you exhaust yourself. Change the belief at the root and the whole life reorganizes.

Most coaching tries to install a new program on top of the old one. I don't do that. My work is closer to archaeology — removing the layers that were never really yours, until what was always there underneath becomes legible again.

Four Pillars

What I draw from.

The method isn't borrowed from a single book. It's a synthesis of four traditions I've lived inside, each speaking to a different layer of how a person actually changes.

01

The Psychology of Change

The practical mechanics of transformation: how beliefs form, why they persist, and what it actually takes to rewire the chain from belief to result.

02

Plant-Based Medicine

The body holds what the mind narrates. I work with the nervous system and the wisdom of natural healing to loosen what's stuck below language.

03

Jewish Philosophy

An ancient grammar for meaning, responsibility, and the examined life — wrestling honestly with who you are and what you're here to do.

04

Kabbalah & Energy

The map beneath the map: how energy moves through a life, where it gets blocked, and how to bring the hidden structure of the self back into flow.

The Approach

How I work.

I work with a small number of people at a time. That's on purpose — this isn't volume work. Whether you move through the self-paced program or work with me one-on-one, the aim is the same: to take you to the root, not just the surface, and to leave you with an orientation you can sustain long after we're done.
